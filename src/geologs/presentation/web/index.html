            Import & export

            Bring in history from another app, or take yours with you.

            Export

            Every location you recorded in these days, oldest first, timed in your timezone (GPX in UTC, as GPX apps expect). Leave the dates empty for all of them. A CSV or GeoJSON file can be imported again.

            Full backup

            Your whole archive, to move it to another GeoLogs server: every location, saved places and their names, your corrections to stays and travel modes, learned routes, reminders, daily summaries, settings, and the tokens your phones and apps send with, so they keep working once pointed at the new address. Your password and two-step sign-in stay here: make your account on the new server, then import this file there.

            Import

            A CSV with timestamp, lat and lon columns; a GeoJSON file of timed points; Google Takeout's Records.json or Semantic Location History; the Timeline file your phone exports (its Timeline settings → Export Timeline data); or a GeoLogs full backup, which keeps its own tracker names. Locations already here are recognised, so importing a file twice is safe.
