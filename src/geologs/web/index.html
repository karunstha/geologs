      Connect a tracker app

      Phones send locations straight here, with no polling. Use an address your phone can reach, such as HTTPS or a private network like Tailscale.

      Give each device its own tokenCreate one under Settings → Access → Device tokens. Until then, apps need your main access token, which can also read your whole history.

      Stays & stops

      When time in one place counts as a visit, and when a short stop on the way is worth keeping.

      Missed a stop, or found one that wasn't real?In History, select a trip to add a stop on it, or a stay to remove it. Your changes are kept when stays are recalculated.

      Saving recalculates stays and trips across your whole history, which can take a moment.

      Movement

      How speed is read from your devices, and which speeds mean walking, running, cycling, or driving.

      Activity speedsA trip is labelled by its typical (median) speed, in km/h.

      Changing activity speeds recalculates trips across your whole history. Travel modes you set by hand are kept.

      Place names

      The service that turns coordinates into names for stays, places, and search.

      Provider

      Mapbox allows Search Box results for temporary use only. GeoLogs keeps place names in its lookup cache, saved places, and daily summaries, which goes beyond those terms.

      After switching, names are looked up again with the new provider as you view them. Your own name corrections always win.
