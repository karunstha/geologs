    Search

    Place names

    Choose the service that turns coordinates into useful names.

    Mapbox allows its Search Box results for temporary use only. GeoLogs stores place names in its lookup cache, saved places, and daily summaries, which goes beyond those terms.

    Names stays and visited places, labels your current location, and powers place search. After switching, names are looked up again with the new provider as you view them.

    Sources

    Connect a tracker app

    Phones send their location straight here; no polling needed.

    Use the server address your phone can reach (HTTPS or a private network such as Tailscale). Authenticate with GEOLOGS_INGEST_TOKEN: it can send locations but not read them.

    Daily archive

    Summaries

    Review records created after each completed day.

    The completed day is summarized after midnight in your configured timezone. Each record includes places, trips, distance, activity, location fixes, and reminder activity.

    These structured records are also available through the MCP daily overview tools.

    Add place

    Search near your latest fix or enter coordinates.

    A saved place is a zone. Visits inside it keep the name of the building or place you were at, and the zone adds up your time there and fires arrival reminders. Home is the exception: everything inside it is Home.
