    Place names & search

    Mapbox allows its Search Box results for temporary use only. Geotracker stores place names in its lookup cache, saved places, and daily summaries, which goes beyond those terms.

    Names stays and visited places, labels your current location, and powers place search. After switching, names are looked up again with the new provider as you view them.

    Daily summaries

    The completed day is summarized after midnight in the configured timezone. Each record includes places and observed time, including Home, trips, distance, fixes, activity, and reminder activity.

    These structured records are available through the MCP daily overview tools.
